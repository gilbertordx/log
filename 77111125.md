## 77

77 is the sum of three consecutive squares:

**4² + 5² + 6² = 16 + 25 + 36 = 77**

## 25

25 is a **square number**, being **5² = 25**.

25 is also the smallest square that is also a sum of two nonzero squares:

**25 = 3² + 4²**

In other words, the numbers **3, 4, and 5** form the smallest **Pythagorean triple**.

## 1,111

1,111 is an **odd composite semiprime** number composed of two different prime numbers multiplied together.

**1,111 = 11 × 101**

It has a total of **four divisors**: 1, 11, 101, and 1,111.
